Add --fill-pct for --consolidate

Will add IPs (up to a percentage of total IP addresses to consolidate
into larger CIDRs)

$ ./grepnet 192.0.2.1-192.0.2.2 --consolidate --fill-pct=25
$ ./grepnet 192.0.2.1-192.0.2.2 --consolidate --fill-pct=50
192.0.2.0/30



Add --ipv4-sorter-than, --ipv4-longer-than, --ipv6-sorter-than,
and --ipv6-longer-than
For use with --extract and --consolidate 

.. shorter/longer than, not equal to.

$ echo "192.0.2.0/25 192.0.2.0/29 192.0.2.1 10.0.0.0/8" | \
  ./grepnet --extract --ipv4-shorter-than=29 192.0.2.0/24
192.0.2.0/25
$ echo "192.0.2.0/25 192.0.2.1 10.0.0.0/8" | \
  ./grepnet --extract --ipv4-shorter-than=29
192.0.2.0/25
10.0.0.0/8
$ 

Add --exclude-cidr-behaviour={subset,superset,equal,related,cut}
    with sanity chechs, cut and related can are not cumilative 

Cut only relates to --extract and --consolidate; and should error if 
used without those.

$ echo 192.0.2.0/24 | ./grepnet --extract \
  --exclude-cidr=192.0.2.0/30             \
  --exclude-cidr-behaviour=cut
